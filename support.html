Common questions

Does the app work offline?

Yes. Everything works fully offline. There is no account, no ads, and no tracking.

Where is my journal and my saved du'as stored?

On your device only. They are never uploaded anywhere. Deleting the app removes them. See our Privacy Policy.

How do I add the Name of the Day widget?

Touch and hold an empty area of your Home or Lock Screen, tap the + button, search for “99 Names,” and choose a widget size.

A reflection, du'a, or reference looks off. Can I report it?

Please do. The reflections, du'as, and detailed meanings are written for this app to aid reflection, not as quotations, and we welcome corrections from qualified reviewers. Email us the Name and what you noticed.
