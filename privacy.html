Privacy Policy

99 Names: Asma ul Husna · Last updated 29 September 2026

This app does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. Everything you create stays on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. 99 Names of Allah has no account system, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking SDKs. The app makes no network calls.

What stays on your device

Your favorites, private journal reflections, the du'as you write, and your appearance and notification settings are stored locally on your device using the system's standard local storage. They are never uploaded to us or to any third party. If you delete the app, this data is removed with it.

Your phone's own backup (iCloud or iTunes backup on iPhone, Android's device backup on Android) may include this data in your personal backup, under your account. We never receive it.

Notifications

If you turn on the optional daily or evening reminder, notifications are scheduled locally on your device by your phone's operating system (iOS or Android). No reminder data leaves your device.

Children's privacy

The app is rated suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
