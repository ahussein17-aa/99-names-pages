A note on accuracy

The Arabic, transliterations, and short meanings follow the well-established list of the Asma ul Husna. The reflections, du’as, and detailed meanings were written for this app to help you reflect. They are not quotations, and they are not presented as such.

Qur’an references carry their status openly: those still awaiting scholarly review are marked as such inside the app rather than passed off as settled. The full list of what the app draws on is on its Sources & References page. Please confirm any reference with a qualified scholar before relying on it in teaching or da’wah.
